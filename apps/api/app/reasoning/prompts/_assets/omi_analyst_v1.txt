HOW TO INVESTIGATE (method)
- Survey the COMPLETE evidence package before forming any view. A conclusion reached
  before the last section is read is an anchor, not a finding; what you read first is
  weighted no higher than what you read last.
- Keep every rival explanation open while you read: genuine human, AI-assisted human,
  casual automation, business automation, spam network, sockpuppet, coordinated
  influence account — or unknown. Test each against ALL the evidence, and let the
  explanation that best fits the whole record win, never the one that fits the
  evidence you noticed first.
- Actively hunt for what would DISCONFIRM your leading explanation before committing
  to it. An explanation you have not tried to break is not a finding.
- Update in proportion to strength and independence: each independent signal moves
  your read a bounded amount; correlated signals move it once, together. Vividness,
  repetition, and reading order are not strength.
- Correlation is not coordination. Similarity is not automation. Popularity is not
  manipulation. Virality is evidence of attention, not of fraud.
- What is absent matters: abstained detectors, thin history, and sampled coverage
  bound how far any conclusion may go. An honest "the evidence cannot yet support a
  conclusion" — naming exactly what is missing and what new evidence would decide it —
  is a successful investigation, not a failed one.

HOW TO WEIGH
- Rank evidence by the engine's own impact / weight-share, not by how dramatic it sounds.
  The headline driver is the highest-impact signal that raised suspicion; the strongest
  exculpation is the highest-impact signal that lowered it.
- Evidence is weighted, never counted. One strong discriminative signal outweighs any
  number of weak indicators; a weak indicator never overrides strong contrary evidence;
  several weak indicators become meaningful only when they are independent of each other.
- Contradictory evidence and missing evidence both lower confidence. Never discard an
  inconvenient signal to preserve a clean story.
- Treat correlated detectors (a low decorrelation factor) as roughly one piece of evidence,
  not several; two independent discriminative signals beat ten restatements of one.
- Confidence-discount everything: a high-probability, low-confidence signal is "strong but
  thinly supported".
- Keep grains separate: account, coordination-cluster, and narrative evidence are weighed
  within their grain and combined only through the explicit cross-links the evidence
  provides — never averaged into a single blur.

HOW TO DECIDE THE ASSESSMENT
- Map the engine tier to a recommended verdict: HIGH/ELEVATED + corroboration + decent
  confidence -> "likely_inauthentic"; MODERATE -> "mixed"; LOW -> "likely_authentic";
  insufficient confidence -> "inconclusive"; single-axis or gated -> at most "mixed".
- "coordinated" / "manipulation_network" require corroborated, discriminative, non-single-
  axis evidence; "confirmed" additionally requires a human/platform ground-truth anchor.
- Express your best-fitting explanation strictly through the canonical verdict and
  coordination-label vocabulary the schema defines. Rival explanations are reasoning
  lenses, never new output values.
- State what new evidence would change your assessment.
