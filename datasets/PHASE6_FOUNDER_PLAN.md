# Phase 6 — Founder Action Plan: First Real-User Validation

> **Status:** planning only. This document does **not** authorize building any
> Phase 6 system (no recruitment CRM, no in-app onboarding flows, no growth
> instrumentation beyond the Phase 4 five-question learning loop already shipped).
> It is the founder's manual playbook for taking Omi to its first 25 real users.

## 0. The one objective

Move Omi from **built** to **validated**. We have a working magic moment —
*"a coordinated account-group surfaced with its evidence, and its counter-evidence,
already assembled"* — and a cold-visitor path to it in ~10 seconds
(`/` CTA → `/rc/cmp_feat_cn_xinjiang`). We do **not** yet have proof that a real
researcher, with a real case on their desk, finds that painkiller worth their time.

**The single number that matters:** how many of 25 hand-picked users bring a
*case of their own* (not the demo) through Omi within three weeks. Everything
below serves that number. Timebox: **3 weeks** of founder-led outreach + conversations.

**Do NOT, during validation:** build new features mid-loop, chase a public launch
(HN / ProductHunt), widen the ICP before one is proven, or count signups/likes as
progress. Politeness is not validation.

---

## 1. Target user profiles

Three ICPs, sharpest fit first. Over-index on A.

### A. OSINT researchers / disinformation analysts  *(primary — sharpest fit)*
- **Who:** independent researchers, DFRLab-style analysts, think-tank fellows,
  Stanford Internet Observatory diaspora, ASPI / EU DisinfoLab / Bellingcat
  contributors, the "is this network coordinated?" thread-writers.
- **Job to be done:** prove a cluster of accounts is coordinated, with evidence
  defensible to an editor or peer reviewer.
- **Why Omi's magic moment lands:** the *counter-evidence assembly* is exactly what
  makes their finding survive scrutiny — that's the part they currently do by hand,
  badly, under deadline.
- **Current pain / alternative:** manual network mapping in Gephi / spreadsheets /
  hand-built timelines; hours per cluster; no reproducibility.
- **How to recognize one:** posts account screenshots + "here's the network";
  vocabulary = coordination, astroturfing, ban evasion, inauthentic behavior.

### B. Investigative journalists on the platform / influence beat  *(secondary)*
- **Who:** reporters with a misinformation/tech beat; freelancers pitching
  influence-op stories.
- **JTBD:** a fast, defensible "organic or coordinated?" call *before* publishing.
- **Why Omi lands:** the shareable public report (`/rc/...`) is something they can
  link in a piece or hand an editor as backup.
- **Pain / alternative:** no tooling of their own; they borrow a friendly academic
  and wait; deadline pressure kills the story.
- **Recognize:** bylines on influence-op / bot-network stories; "sources say"
  coordination claims with thin public evidence.

### C. Trust & Safety / platform-integrity professionals  *(tertiary — narrow but high-intent)*
- **Who:** T&S analysts at small/mid platforms, marketplaces, dating/community apps
  *without* a Meta-scale integrity team.
- **JTBD:** triage inbound reports of brigading / astroturfing / review-fraud rings.
- **Why Omi lands:** evidence-first output drops cleanly into casework and appeals.
- **Pain / alternative:** crude internal tooling; they buy or borrow.
- **Recognize:** TSPA membership; job titles with "integrity," "platform abuse,"
  "T&S operations."

## 3. Outreach channels (ranked by expected response for a cold founder)

1. **Warm intro** — highest conversion. Map your 2nd-degree network; ask one
   person for *one specific* intro, not "anyone who'd be interested."
2. **Direct DM with a live artifact** — do *not* pitch. Send the `/rc` public
   report for a campaign they'd recognize and ask one sharp question. The report
   sells; you ask.
3. **Public reply** — answer a researcher's live "is this coordinated?" thread with
   an Omi report link. Earns inbound and credibility at once.
4. **Targeted personal email** — short, references their specific work, links the
   `/rc` report, single ask.
5. **Value-first community post** — share a genuinely interesting *finding* (not a
   launch) in Bellingcat Discord / r/OSINT.
6. **Avoid for now:** HN/ProductHunt launch, paid ads, cold mass-email. Wrong
   audience, premature, vanity.

## 4. First 25 people — the strategy (a system, not a name list)

You can't pre-name 25 strangers, so build and work the list as a system:

- **Build a 25-row sheet:** `name | profile (A/B/C) | where found | warm path? |
  the specific report I'll send | the one question I'll ask`.
- **Composition:** 12× A (OSINT), 8× B (journalists), 5× C (T&S). Over-index the
  sharpest fit so a clear signal can emerge from one segment.
- **Send in waves of 5**, 2–3 days apart. Adapt each wave's message from what landed
  in the last. Never blast 25 at once — you'd burn the list before learning anything.
- **Personalization is non-negotiable:** every message references something they
  specifically did or published. No template-blasting.
- **Lead with the artifact:** open with a live `/rc` report of a campaign *in their
  domain* (e.g. the featured Xinjiang campaign for a China-watcher). Let the magic
  moment do the work.
- **The ask is a conversation, not a signup:** target a 20-minute call or a reaction
  to the report. At this stage, conversation > conversion.
- **Track per person:** sent → replied → opened report → booked call → *ran their own
  scan*. The Phase 4 public-view metric (Q3) already records whether the shared link
  was actually read.

## 5. What feedback to collect (mapped to the five learning questions)

Use the Phase 4 five questions as the interview spine so feedback is structured, not
vibes:

1. **Value:** "What was your first reaction opening the report? Did it tell you
   something you'd otherwise spend hours assembling?" → did the magic moment land?
2. **Trust:** "Would you cite this in something you publish? What would have to be
   true for you to trust it?" → did the *counter-evidence* earn trust?
3. **Comeback:** "Is there a case on your desk *right now* you'd run through this?"
   → real pull vs. politeness.
4. **Share:** "Who else on your team / beat needs to see this?" → organic distribution.
5. **Pay:** "If this saved you X hours, what's that worth — and who holds the budget?"
   → willingness to pay. Don't skip it; the awkwardness *is* data.

Also capture, every conversation:
- **The single biggest missing thing** that stopped them using Omi for a *real* case.
- **Where they got confused in the first 60 seconds** — this is the live test of the
  Phase 5 Item 1 comprehension guidance. If real users still stall, that's a finding.

## 6. Success criteria that define validation

Sharp, falsifiable, vanity-free. Frame as continue-vs-pivot.

| Signal | Threshold (of 25) | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Real-case pull** ⭐ | ≥ **5** bring a case of their *own* | Strongest signal — they're using it for work, not humoring you |
| Activation | ≥ 40% who open a report **run their own scan** in a week | Magic moment → action |
| Trust | ≥ 3 say unprompted they'd **cite/publish** from it (or do) | The counter-evidence earned trust |
| Distribution | ≥ 3 **share** it with a colleague unprompted (Q3 fires from a new IP) | Organic pull |
| Retention | ≥ 5 run a **second** scan in week 2 with no nudge | Not a one-time novelty |
| WTP | ≥ 3 give a **concrete number** *and* name who holds budget | A market, not a hobby |

**Kill / re-segment signal:** if **< 2** bring a real case after 25 *quality*
conversations, the magic moment is not a painkiller for this ICP — re-segment
(probably narrow to pure-A OSINT) **before** building anything else.

**Explicitly NOT success:** signups, page views, social likes, "this is cool" with
no return scan, or a polite "I'd use this someday." None of those move the one number.

---

## 7. What this plan deliberately does not do

- No recruitment CRM, no email automation, no in-app growth instrumentation — the
  founder runs this by hand for 25 people so the learning is high-fidelity.
- No new product features during the validation window. Feedback is *collected*,
  not *built* on, until the loop closes.
- No ICP widening until one profile clears the bar above.
